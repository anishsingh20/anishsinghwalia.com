### A) adityaoberai.com: "Come spend a minute in my world"
- **What it is**: the homepage is a full-screen interactive **low-poly 3D room** rendered on `<canvas>` (SvelteKit app). It's an explorable diorama rather than a scored game. Built with SvelteKit (`_app/immutable`, `data-sveltekit-preload-data`); the 3D engine isn't visible in the HTML and is likely three.js/Threlte **[unverified]**.
- **Controls**: tap/click the floor to walk, or WASD/arrow keys. Tap an object or press **E** nearby to "look closer". ARIA label and instructions describe this.
- **Objects = navigation**: desk→Work & build, notebook→Writing, camera→Photography, shelf→Collectibles, corkboard→Community & speaking, mirror→About, window→Now, door→Elsewhere & contact.
- **Easter-egg "little things"** carry personality, each with a one-line caption: Squirtle plush, Batman cowl, LEGO DeLorean, Man United flag, suitcase with luggage tags (Yokohama, London, Toronto, Atlanta), football, gramophone (opens a docked music player playing his playlist), bed (opens a Robert Frost poem: "miles to go before I sleep").
- **Accessibility & resilience**: a "Menu" popover lists every object as links. "See it on paper ↗" (/index) gives a classic text portfolio with stats (100+ articles, 8+ talks, 5x Microsoft MVP, CMX award). Before load it shows a still image (room-still.jpg) with an "Opening the room…" card, plus a noscript fallback, skip link, "Room settings" (Reset view, Low power, Less motion), and a warm paper theme (#F8F5ED).
- **SEO**: Person JSON-LD (jobTitle, worksFor, address, sameAs to all socials), canonical, OG image of the room, Twitter card.

### B) bot.danikellogg.com: "Ask About Dani's Career"
- **How it works**: an LLM with RAG, not canned answers. Per her write-up (https://supercrisp.substack.com/p/dani-bot-my-personal-career-chat, 16 Sep 2026), it was **built entirely on DigitalOcean**: App Platform hosts the UI, Agent Platform runs the agent, a Knowledge Base handles retrieval over markdown files stored in Spaces, and the model is gpt-oss-20b. It started from DO's open-source RAG Chat Bot blueprint and was built with Claude Code.
- **Knowledge files (10)**: Identity, Resume, Career story, Positioning, Testimonials, Voice & tone, Side projects, Role-fit, Technical depth, FAQ. Behaviour rules live in Agent Instructions, kept separate from the knowledge base. Guardrails (jailbreak, moderation, PII via Presidio) were tuned so they no longer block her own name, location, and dates. Off-limits topics are compensation, family beyond the basics, and other job talks. When it doesn't know, it says so and offers a direct intro.
- **Front-end**: a small FastAPI app serving one static HTML/JS page. The page POSTs `/api/chat` with `{message, history}`, renders replies with a hand-written markdown renderer, and shows a typing indicator. Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a newline.
- **UI**: dark green-black terminal aesthetic (#0a0f0c bg, #4ecf6c accent), Red Hat Display + JetBrains Mono, dotted-grid background, a header "dani_bot / career_chatbot" with GitHub/LinkedIn/Substack icons and a site link, an "ask_me_anything" badge, a "See how I made this →" banner, and a max-720px chat column.
- **Suggested prompt chips**: "What's it like to work with Dani?", "What do other people say about Dani?", "What roles would she be a good fit for?", "What kinds of companies or products fit her best?", "How technical is she, really?", "Walk me through her career history", "Tell me about a launch she led start to finish".

Copy the 10-file knowledge structure and the honest-fallback "contact Anish" behaviour.
