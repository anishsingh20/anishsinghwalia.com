# Career story

Anish started writing online in 2017, including GeeksforGeeks while he was at VIT, and he studied machine learning early. He then spent the Nutanix years in site reliability: intern, SRE, senior SRE, and a cloud consulting engagement for American Express.

He has said that in 2023 he felt bored and drained, hit reset, joined DigitalOcean as a full-time technical writer in mid-2023, and restarted his Medium blog after a pause from 2020 to 2024. He has said he automated 60 to 70 percent of his workflow.

The ActiveCampaign Autonomous story by Maddy Osman, 21 May 2026, covers his Cursor and DataForSEO content-refresh workflow at DigitalOcean. Turnaround fell from about a full day to a few hours, and output rose from 1 or 2 updated pieces a day to 4 or 5. Other writers and solutions architects at DigitalOcean adopted it. https://autonomous.activecampaign.com/stories/scalable-content-refresh-workflow
